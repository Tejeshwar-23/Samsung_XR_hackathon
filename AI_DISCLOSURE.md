# AI Disclosure Statement

**Project:** Degradation-Aware IMU Dead-Reckoning for Extended Reality
**Competition:** Samsung XR Hackathon 2026 – Track 03: Path Finder

## AI Tools Used

- ChatGPT (OpenAI)
- Google Gemini through Antigravity

## Areas of AI Assistance

AI tools assisted with technical guidance, algorithm discussions, code suggestions and modifications, debugging, regression-test development, experiment analysis, documentation, diagrams and presentation preparation.

## Human Oversight and Verification

All AI-assisted suggestions and code changes were reviewed by the project team. The team made the final engineering decisions and conducted local testing, regression testing and benchmark verification.

## AI Usage During Runtime

The submitted estimator uses a deterministic, physics-based Python inertial navigation pipeline. ChatGPT and Gemini are not required during execution or inference. No externally trained generative AI model is used by the production estimator.

## Declaration

We acknowledge the use of AI tools as development assistants and have disclosed their involvement transparently. The project team is responsible for the submitted implementation, experimental evaluation and reported results.
